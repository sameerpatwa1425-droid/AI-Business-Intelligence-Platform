# AI Business Intelligence & Decision Support Platform

## Overview

The AI Business Intelligence & Decision Support Platform is a full-stack analytics application designed to help businesses monitor performance, analyze sales data, and generate actionable insights.

The platform collects sales records, processes business metrics, and presents them through an interactive dashboard featuring charts, KPIs, rankings, and AI-generated business insights.

---

## Objectives

* Track revenue and profit performance
* Analyze category-wise sales contribution
* Identify top-performing products
* Visualize business trends
* Generate AI-driven business insights
* Support managerial decision-making
